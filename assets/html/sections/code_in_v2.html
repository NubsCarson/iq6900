3. IF IT STALLS, NOTHING IS LOST

solana congestion or a weak RPC can stall an upload, but nothing is lost: an interrupted upload resumes from where it stopped - already-landed chunks are skipped, so RETRY only sends what is missing. your funds stay in your session account and are refunded if a write gives up.

2. WRITES GO THROUGH YOUR WALLET'S RPC

your wallet broadcasts every write through the RPC it has saved for robinhood chain (4663); the site's RPC setting only serves reads. if every write fails instantly (-32603), that saved RPC is dead: edit the network in your wallet and point it at the official rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com.

3. IF IT STALLS, ALMOST NOTHING IS SPENT

the storage fee charges once, at the final tx - an interrupted upload cost gas pennies at most (gas on robinhood chain is fractions of a cent). there is no session account to strand funds in: your wallet holds everything the whole time, and RETRY starts a fresh write.
